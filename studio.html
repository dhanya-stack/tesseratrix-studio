Our Story

Tesseratrix began as an independent initiative to bring ideas to life through technology.

Rooted in mechanical engineering and inspired by curiosity, the studio explores how engineering, design and software can come together to create useful digital experiences.

From everyday applications to interactive educational platforms, games and engineering simulations, Tesseratrix is built around one idea: curiosity should lead to creation.

The Founder

Tesseratrix was founded by a Chartered Mechanical Engineer with over 14 years of experience across advanced engineering, semiconductor technology and research.

The studio combines engineering experience with a growing focus on software development, artificial intelligence and interactive technologies.

Our Approach

  • Thoughtful engineering
  • Simplicity and accessibility
  • Curiosity and exploration
  • Practical, meaningful products

Our Direction

Tesseratrix is currently an independent, founder-led studio developing its own products. Its longer-term ambition includes collaborations and custom technology solutions.
