The studio

Curiosity leads to creation.

Tesseratrix is an independent, founder-led software and engineering studio building thoughtful digital experiences across disciplines.

Our Story

Tesseratrix Studio began with a simple impulse: to take ideas beyond the notebook and turn them into something people can use. What started as independent explorations in software, engineering and learning is developing into a home for purposeful digital products.

The studio is deliberately multidisciplinary. A question about physics might become an interactive simulation; a frustrating everyday task might inspire an application; a subject worth understanding might become a learning platform. Curiosity connects all of them.

Tesseratrix is not built around a single product or a linear development roadmap. Several curiosity-driven ideas evolve in parallel, often informing one another across applications, learning platforms, games, simulations and engineering tools. The studio will continue developing and sharing these interconnected projects, refining existing work while exploring new possibilities. As it grows, Tesseratrix also hopes to collaborate with others on practical digital experiences and custom technology solutions.

The Founder

Tesseratrix Studio was founded by Dhanya Nair, a Chartered Mechanical Engineer with more than 14 years of experience across advanced engineering, semiconductor technology and research. Her background in designing and developing complex engineering systems informs the studio’s practical, systems-oriented approach to creating software, simulations and interactive learning experiences.

The vision is to create useful, thoughtfully designed technology that makes complex ideas more approachable. Through Tesseratrix, Dhanya brings engineering discipline together with curiosity about software, science, learning and creativity. The aim is to build experiences that invite people to explore, understand and make things for themselves.

What We Create

Our work spans practical applications, digital learning platforms, educational games, simulations and engineering-focused tools. Some projects are available online, while others are in active development.

We believe useful technology should be approachable, whether it helps someone concentrate, plan, learn or explore complex engineering concepts.

How We Work

We begin by understanding the problem and the people who might use the result. Then we explore, prototype, test and refine. Engineering discipline brings structure and reliability; design brings clarity and approachability; curiosity keeps us experimenting.

Our goal is to make technology understandable, accessible and genuinely useful, from a simple timer to a detailed interactive simulation.
