Our Story

Tesseratrix began as an independent initiative to transform curiosity into useful technology.

With roots in mechanical engineering and a growing interest in software, AI, interactive learning and simulation, the studio explores ideas across disciplines.

What We Create

Applications, educational platforms, games, interactive simulations and engineering-focused digital tools.

Our Approach

  • Thoughtful engineering
  • Simplicity and accessibility
  • Practical usefulness
  • Creative exploration

The Founder

Tesseratrix was founded by a Chartered Mechanical Engineer with over 14 years of experience in advanced engineering, semiconductor technology and research.

The studio keeps the founder’s identity privacy-conscious while bringing that broad experience into product development.

Looking Ahead

Tesseratrix is currently an independent, founder-led studio developing its own products, with longer-term ambitions for collaborations and custom technology solutions.
